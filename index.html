    Safety tips

    A few simple habits keep every parcel safe, for you, our riders and the person receiving it.

    • Pack it tight

      Use a sturdy box or bag, tape every opening and keep fragile things padded.

    • Label clearly

      Write the recipient’s name, address and phone number on the parcel.

    • Seal food well

      Use airtight containers. Tell us if it is perishable so we can deliver it quickly.

    • Check the order number

      Ask the rider for your order number at pickup. Ours is always shared by phone or WhatsApp.

    • Confirm the price first

      We tell you the total before you confirm. Ask if anything is unclear.

    • Hand over to the right person

      Make sure the recipient knows a parcel is coming and is home to collect it.

    • Share only what is needed

      We need an address and a phone number. Never share bank PINs or OTPs with anyone, including us.

    • Meet the rider at the door

      Seniors and children: let a family member or neighbour be with you at pickup.

    • Call if anything feels off

      Delay, damage or a wrong contact? Ring us at once and we will sort it out.

    Please do not send:
    • Cash, jewellery or other high-value items unless we agree it on the call
    • Medicines without a proper pack and label
    • Anything illegal, flammable, sharp or hazardous

    Quick questions

    Do I need a smartphone?

    No. A basic phone call is enough. WhatsApp updates are optional.

    What can’t you carry?

    Prohibited or hazardous items, and parcels above our size limit.

    Can you carry homemade food?

    Yes, if it is securely packed. Perishables need extra care, so tell us when you call.

    How do I know the price?

    We tell you the total before you confirm.

    What if I am outside the area?

    Call us anyway. We are expanding after the pilot.
